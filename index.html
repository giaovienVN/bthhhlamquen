📈 Periodic Trends: Periods 1 to 3

Understanding periodic trends across Periods 1, 2, and 3 helps predict chemical reactivity, bonding behavior, and physical properties:

1. Atomic Radius (Bán kính nguyên tử)

Decreases across a period (Left → Right): As nuclear charge ($Z$) increases, protons pull electron shells tighter towards the nucleus.
Increases down a group (Top → Bottom): Additional electron shells (Period 1: K, Period 2: K+L, Period 3: K+L+M) shield valence electrons.

2. Electronegativity (Độ âm điện) & Reactivity

Increases across a period: Halogens (Fluorine: 3.98, Chlorine: 3.16) have the highest electronegativity. Noble gases (He, Ne, Ar) have zero electronegativity due to full octet/duet shells.
Decreases down a group: Electrons are further from positive nucleus.

3. Metallic vs. Non-metallic Character

Left side (Groups 1–2, 13): Alkali & alkaline earth metals (Li, Be, Na, Mg, Al) readily donate valence electrons ($1e^-, 2e^-, 3e^-$).
Middle (Group 14): Metalloids like Silicon (Si) and Boron (B) act as semiconductors.
Right side (Groups 15–18): Non-metals (C, N, O, P, S, F, Cl) and noble gases.

🔬 How to Play Periodic Quest
1. Goal: Place all 18 chemical elements (Period 1 to 3: Hydrogen to Argon) into their proper periodic table grid coordinates.

  • Drag & Drop: Drag any card from your deck at the bottom and drop it onto its slot.
  • Click to Select: Click a card in your deck (it lights up with an active neon pulse), then click the target cell on the periodic table.
3. Spatial Scaffolding: Hovering over any slot highlights its corresponding Period row and Group column header to help you locate coordinates.
4. Scoring & Streaks:
  • +100 base points for each correct placement.
  • Consecutive correct placements earn Streak multipliers (2x, 3x, 4x)!
  • Incorrect placements reset your streak and provide an educational hint.
5. Element Inspector: Click on any placed element on the board at any time to open the animated Bohr orbital model and see its full electron configuration, valence shells, and fun facts!
